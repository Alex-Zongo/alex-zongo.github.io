On **October 7, 2026**, I presented **Les modèles vision-langage-action** as part of SEINAR's **Embodied AI (Robot Humanoïde)** panel, alongside **Clena Traoré** and **Aloute Sana**. The session was scheduled for **14:00–15:00** and addressed students interested in designing robots.

My presentation explored how robot policies connect visual observations and language instructions to actions, using **OpenVLA** as a concrete example. It also introduced alternative action-generation approaches, including diffusion policies and flow-based models such as the **π0** family.
